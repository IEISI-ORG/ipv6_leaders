## Slide Deck Structure and Design Recommendations

### Overall Design Principles
- Use consistent visual language throughout slide deck
- Limit text to 5-7 bullet points per slide maximum
- Include relevant visuals, diagrams, and infographics
- Consistent color-coding for different types of information:
  - Business considerations (blue)
  - Technical considerations (green)
  - Strategic considerations (purple)
  - Risks/challenges (amber)

## Interactive Discussion Details

### Interactive Discussion #1: "IPv6 Readiness Assessment"
**Format:** Small group exercise (4-5 participants per group)
**Materials Needed:**
- IPv6 Readiness Assessment worksheets
- Current state analysis templates
- Readiness scoring rubric

**Process:**
1. Groups assess their organization's current IPv6 readiness across dimensions:
   - Technical infrastructure
   - Staff knowledge and skills
   - Business case understanding
   - Executive support
   - Security preparedness
   - Resource availability

2. Groups identify top 3 strengths and top 3 gaps in their readiness
3. Each group creates a brief presentation sharing key insights
4. Facilitator captures common themes across organizations

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Baseline understanding of readiness gaps
- Peer learning about common challenges
- Priority areas for immediate attention identified

### Interactive Discussion #2: "Deployment Roadmap Development"
**Format:** Facilitated planning exercise (individual work with group sharing)
**Materials Needed:**
- Deployment roadmap templates
- First 90-days planning worksheet
- Stakeholder communication plan template

**Process:**
1. Participants draft initial high-level roadmap focusing on first 90 days
2. Particular attention to:
   - Executive sponsorship and stakeholder engagement
   - Initial assessment and planning activities
   - Skills development priorities
   - Early wins and proof points
   - Resource requirements

3. Small group sharing of approaches
4. Facilitator-led discussion of common elements and differentiation points

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Draft roadmap for immediate next steps
- Clarity on immediate priorities
- Shared understanding of implementation approaches
